# Hematoxylin from an H&E image

> [!abstract] Summary
> Watch light pass through stained tissue on an H&E slide. This interactive walkthrough breaks down how transmitted RGB pixel intensities are converted into optical density via the Beer-Lambert law, how stain absorption vectors are estimated, and how hematoxylin is separated from eosin to quantify nuclear contrast.
